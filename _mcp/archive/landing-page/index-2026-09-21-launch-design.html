Find a time to talk

03 / DESIGN

Calculate statistical power

“What could a study with 60 schools detect?”

Power, sample size, minimum detectable effects, costs and sensitivity scenarios using my existing calculator. Results expose assumptions and link to the scenario; R and Stata exports are available.

04 / CONNECTIONS

Explore social capital

“Which countries are most connected to Guatemala?”

Country-level Social Connectedness Index, plus Social Capital Atlas records for US counties, ZIP codes, colleges and high schools. Sources and definitions travel with the numbers.

05 / MOVEMENT

Understand migration

“Show Colombia → US migration in 2022.”

Monthly, residence-based migration estimates for 2019–2022. Query a country pair or compare destinations, with coverage, provenance and access to the source release.

06 / EVIDENCE

Find AI research and guidance

“What evidence is there on AI helping teachers?”

A curated starting library of public study summaries and J-PAL / PAIE guidance. Completed findings and research in progress are labeled. This is not an exhaustive literature search.

07 / DEVELOP

Turn an idea into evaluation questions

“We’re piloting an AI job coach. How could we evaluate it?”

A starting scaffold for your assistant: theory of change, outcomes, comparison groups, implementation questions, power assumptions and relevant public guidance. Local knowledge and research judgment fill in the details.

Public by design.

This service exposes public research and aggregate datasets. It has no access to my email, private calendars, internal documents or individual research records. Calculations run here; interpretation happens in your assistant. Please keep queries free of sensitive personal information.

Data are versioned snapshots, not live feeds. Missing observations stay missing. Use the returned definitions and limitations when interpreting a result.
