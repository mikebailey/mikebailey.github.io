The **Project on AI and Evidence (PAIE)** is an initiative at [J-PAL](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/) at MIT that identifies, evaluates, and scales innovative applications of AI for social good and in the fight against poverty, bringing together AI adopters, tech companies, and researchers. I joined J-PAL as Senior Advisor in July 2026 to work on this portfolio across research, policy, and practice.

PAIE funds rigorous research &mdash; largely randomized evaluations &mdash; on AI applications, builds practical guidance for evaluating AI-based programs, and develops capacity among researchers, governments, and NGOs. Focus sectors include education, health, labor markets, climate change, and financial inclusion.
